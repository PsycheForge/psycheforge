# Jupiter DAO Net-Zero Emissions Proposal

**Author:** Kash
**Date:** February 13, 2026
**Forum:** Jupiter Research Discussion

---

## TL;DR

The proposal aims to achieve net-zero emissions for $JUP tokens by addressing three emission sources through a single DAO vote with two options.

---

## 1. The State of $JUP

Jupiter has demonstrated commitment to token support through:
- Burning 3 billion tokens (30% of team allocation and strategic reserves)
- Team token locks, including founder locks through 2030
- Allocating 50% of onchain revenues to open market buybacks
- Alignment between team and community economics through $JUP currency

The proposal responds to token-holder concerns about emissions in current market conditions.

---

## 2. Sources of Emissions

Three key emission sources are identified:

1. **Jupuary** – Annual airdrop rewarding loyal users/stakers with governance power
2. **Team Vesting** – Token deliveries to team members for ecosystem growth incentive alignment
3. **Mercurial Stakeholders Vesting** – 5% of total supply distributed to Mercurial token holders and investors

---

## 3. Going Green (Stopping Emissions)

### Option 2 Components:

**Jupuary Postponement:**
- Return all 700 million tokens to Community Cold Multisig
- Preserve user snapshot for future distribution
- Revisit when market conditions improve

**Team Emissions Pause:**
- Cease onchain token emissions to team members
- Provide "JUP credits" against Jupiter balance sheet instead
- Jupiter absorbs team member token sales directly

**Mercurial Offset:**
- Accelerate Mercurial stakeholder vesting
- Track approximately 300 wallets holding "99% of allocated $JUP"
- Purchase equivalent tokens for Jupiter treasury when tracked wallets sell or move tokens to exchanges
- Continue offsetting throughout the year

**Net Impact:** Reduces emissions from approximately 1.2 billion to effectively zero tokens.

---

## 4. Proposal Logistics

**Vote Structure:** Two options presented

**Option 1 – Continue with Jupuary:**
- Airdrop checker released ~1 week after vote conclusion
- Distribution commences ~2 weeks after, beginning with 200M $JUP
- Bonus pool (200M) and Jupnet incentives (300M) deploy as planned

**Option 2 – Net-Zero Emissions:**
- Jupuary tokens returned to Community Cold Multisig
- Team Reserve emissions paused indefinitely
- Jupiter absorbs team member token sales
- Mercurial stakeholders airdropped with offset purchases

**Timeline:** Public comment period followed by vote February 17-21, 2026

**ASR Status:** Annual Staking Rewards continue unchanged at 50 million per quarter, regardless of vote outcome or participation

---

## 5. Key Rationale

- Market conditions have changed significantly since original 2024 vote
- Only 29% of current stakers participated in prior Jupuary vote
- "Half measures don't move markets" – bundling required for decisive market signal
- 50/50 approach with community (reflecting original distribution split)
- Preserves snapshot records for future DAO decisions on postponed tokens
- Demonstrates "enormous conviction in the value of the $JUP"

---

## 6. FAQs Summary

**Returned Jupuary Tokens:** Secured in Community Cold Multisig, inaccessible without future DAO vote

**Mercurial Offset Mechanics:** Track <300 known wallets; purchase matching amounts when sales occur on-chain or at CEX

**Team Emissions Work:** Credit system against Jupiter balance sheet; Jupiter becomes counterparty for any sales

**ParaFi Voting:** Locked $35M token allocation unavailable for governance
