# MIP15: Activate the Protocol Fees Flow to the Marinade Foundation and Grant 100M MNDE to Marinade Labs for Operations

**Author:** helloiamvu
**Date:** August 18, 2025, 2:55pm
**Category:** Governance

---

## Summary

This proposal requests DAO approval to activate protocol fee flows into the Marinade Foundation and allocate 100M MNDE from the DAO treasury to Marinade Labs for operational expenses over 12 to 18 months.

Starting in September, all protocol revenues—including fees, buybacks, and burns—will accumulate directly in the DAO treasury. This restructuring strengthens DAO sustainability, enhances MNDE economics, and increases governance authority.

The Marinade team will cease receiving direct protocol revenue access. This proposal provides operational funding during the transition to the new fee model, with expectations that the DAO treasury will naturally replenish through ongoing fee flows and buybacks.

---

## Background

Previously, the team wallet received protocol revenues for operational expenses. Under MIP-11 and MIP-13, the revenue structure changes:

- 50% of performance fees support MNDE buybacks into the DAO treasury
- Remaining fees and burns further strengthen MNDE value
- The DAO treasury becomes the centralized protocol revenue recipient

This represents positive change: buybacks and burns increase MNDE scarcity, and DAO-controlled revenues strengthen governance legitimacy. However, this creates a funding gap—the team requires direct DAO funding.

---

## Proposal

**Amount:** 100M MNDE from the DAO treasury

**Duration:** 12 to 18 months of operational runway

**Use of Funds:**
- Core contributor compensation
- Product development and audits
- Marketing, partnerships, and ecosystem growth
- Community programs and governance operations

**Accountability:**
- Quarterly reporting to the DAO on spending, milestones, and treasury inflows
- Unused MNDE at period conclusion returns to the DAO treasury

---

## Updated: 1-Year Cliff

Based on community feedback, this proposal includes a 1-year cliff on the 100M MNDE allocation.

This ensures:
- No sudden circulating supply increase
- No immediate sell pressure
- DAO treasury replenishment through fees and buybacks before token unlock

The cliff provides MNDE holder assurance while maintaining operational runway for Marinade Labs under the new DAO-funded model.

---

## Rationale

- **Ecosystem positive:** All protocol revenues now strengthen MNDE and the DAO treasury for long-term sustainability
- **Self-funding:** Treasury replenishment occurs over time through fee flows and buybacks
- **Aligned incentives:** DAO oversight ensures accountability while the team focuses on growth and security
- **Proven track record:** Past MNDE allocations demonstrated measurable TVL and ecosystem growth

---

## Conclusion

This proposal activates the next evolution phase: fees and buybacks flow into the DAO treasury, which then funds the team driving growth and innovation.

Approval secures 12 to 18 months of operations while ensuring sustainability, transparency, and DAO interest alignment.

"Delegates are encouraged to vote YES" to maintain momentum and strengthen the MNDE ecosystem.
