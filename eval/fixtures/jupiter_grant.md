# Proposal: Community Growth & Education Working Group (CGEWG) — Q3 2026 Funding

**Author:** @daoScribe (community lead)
**Date:** 2026-04-10
**Ask:** 250,000 USDC from Jupiter DAO treasury

## TL;DR

Fund a 3-person working group for 6 months to grow Jupiter's educational content footprint across X, YouTube, and regional communities (LATAM, SEA).

## Motivation

Jupiter's user base has grown significantly over the past year but onboarding content remains thin outside of English. The CGEWG will produce educational content, host regional AMAs, and support moderation in underrepresented locales.

## Budget

| Line item | Amount (USDC) |
|---|---|
| 3 × FT contributors (6 months) | 180,000 |
| Content production (video/design) | 40,000 |
| Regional event stipends | 20,000 |
| Buffer/ops | 10,000 |
| **Total** | **250,000** |

Disbursement: 50% upfront on passage, 50% after a 3-month review by Core team.

## Success Metrics

The CGEWG will aim to:

- Meaningfully grow Jupiter's educational reach
- Build active regional communities
- Support long-term user retention

We will share regular updates with the DAO via the forum.

## Team

- @daoScribe (lead) — active in Jupiter community since 2023, prior WG lead at another Solana DAO
- @regionalOne (LATAM) — runs a Spanish-language Solana Discord with ~12k members
- @seaOps (SEA) — community contributor across Jupiter and Kamino forums

## Timeline

- Month 1: Hire regional freelancers, publish content calendar
- Months 2–3: Execute content + 2 regional AMAs
- Month 3 review: mid-point checkpoint with Core team
- Months 4–6: Continue execution, publish end-of-WG report

## Appendix: Requested permissions

None beyond the USDC transfer to the CGEWG multisig (addresses in comment below).
